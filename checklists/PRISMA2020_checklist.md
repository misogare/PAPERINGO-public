# PRISMA 2020 checklist

Reporting checklist for the cross-study synthesis these materials support (in preparation). 'Where reported' refers to sections of that synthesis and its supplementary material.

| # | PRISMA 2020 item | Status | Where, or why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title identifies the report as a systematic review | Partial | The title names a cross-study synthesis rather than a systematic review, because the design is not PRISMA-compliant; the design is named in the abstract and in Section 2 |
| 2 | Abstract | Partial | Structured abstract with objective, methods, results and discussion; it does not follow the 12-item PRISMA-for-Abstracts layout |
| 3 | Rationale | Met | Section 1 |
| 4 | Objectives | Met | Abstract Objective; Section 1, closing paragraph |
| 5 | Eligibility criteria | Partial | Appendix B states the population, window, document types and the deduplication and out-of-scope rules; they were applied as an operating rule rather than pre-registered, and no formal PICO frame was used |
| 6 | Information sources | Partial | Appendix B: PubMed, ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore; window 2021 to 2026; corpus closed September 2026. The date each source was last searched is not reported: the PubMed run is attested to March 2026, and the ScienceDirect and IEEE Xplore execution dates were not logged |
| 7 | Search strategy | **Not met** | The base query is reported and attested (Appendix B), but the executed per-database strings, filter states, dates and hit counts were not logged and cannot be reconstructed; contemporaneous logging is a required change for any future acquisition |
| 8 | Selection process | **Not met** | Single operator assisted by deterministic tooling; no dual independent screening, so no inter-screener agreement statistic exists (Appendix B; Section 8.3) |
| 9 | Data collection process | Partial | Automated full-text extraction into a fixed 31-field schema followed by structured coding; single operator, no dual independent extraction; the model-assisted verification was checked by a blinded self-audit by the same adjudicator, not by an independent reader (Section 2). Appendix H.2 tabulates, stage by stage, what automation did, what the human did and where final authority sat |
| 10a | Data items: outcomes | Partial | The synthesis unit is the cross-study claim rather than a pooled outcome; the principal coded fields are named in Appendix B and defined, with their controlled vocabularies, in Appendix C.1; the full schema will be released with the other materials (item 27) |
| 10b | Data items: other variables | Partial | The principal registry variables and their controlled vocabularies are defined in Appendix C.1, and missing values are coded "not reported", never inferred (Appendix B); the full field list and the extraction template are not reproduced in the review and will be released with the other materials (item 27) |
| 11 | Study risk of bias assessment | **Not met** | No design-specific instrument (QUADAS-2, PROBAST, RoB 2, ROBIS) was applied. Appendix G reports the appraisal-relevant characteristics coded for every cited study, and Appendix D transcribes the risk-of-bias ratings that source syntheses report for themselves; neither is a substitute (Section 8.3) |
| 12 | Effect measures | Not applicable | No effect sizes are pooled. Metric values are recorded only with their metric type attached, which is what makes the incommensurability findings of Section 5 possible |
| 13a | Synthesis: eligibility for each synthesis | Met | Section 2 and Appendix A; Appendix H.1 tabulates the admission rule, independence requirement and adjudication rule for each claim type |
| 13b | Synthesis: data preparation | Met | Appendix B coding; Appendix C.1 normalisation rules and coding corrections; Supplementary Note S6 for the record-level corrections |
| 13c | Synthesis: tabulation and visual display | Met | Figures 1 to 3; Tables 1 to 4; Appendix Figure C1; Appendix Tables A1, C1 and D1; the Appendix A record map. SWiM item 7 |
| 13d | Synthesis: methods of synthesis | Not applicable as meta-analysis; met as SWiM | No pooling. The synthesis method is claim-level adjudication with deterministic comparability gates and method- and dataset-family independence checks (Section 2), set out as a decision table in Appendix H.1. SWiM items 1 to 4 |
| 13e | Synthesis: heterogeneity investigation | Met | Section 5 treats cross-study disagreement as the object of study rather than as heterogeneity to be pooled away. SWiM item 5 |
| 13f | Synthesis: sensitivity analyses | Partial | One sensitivity analysis is reported, on the performance-suspect exclusion rule (Section 3.5); there is no sensitivity analysis over the adjudication thresholds themselves |
| 14 | Reporting bias assessment | **Not met** | No funnel-plot, trim-and-fill or selection-model analysis. The corpus-level analogue is recorded in the gap ledger (record ACTOR-G-137) rather than among the headline problems of Section 7: a registry census in the corpus found that only 216 of 2,609 MCI trials registered in the WHO ICTRP (8.28%, still-recruiting trials included) recorded any dissemination of results (Sun et al. 2026, P-1169), so the published trials that the intervention syntheses pool may be a selected fraction of those registered |
| 15 | Certainty assessment | Partial | Table 4 grades each overarching finding strongly supported, moderately supported or scope-bounded with its basis and its principal boundary; the labels are the review's own, describe cross-study support rather than certainty of effect, and are deliberately not GRADE. SWiM item 6 |
| 16a | Results: study selection flow | Partial | Appendix B and Figure C1, upper tier: 1,396 full-text records harvested, 70 removed at deduplication, 8 out of scope, 1,318 included; the flow starts at the harvested records because the number of records the searches returned was not logged |
| 16b | Results: studies excluded, with reasons | Partial | Exclusions are reported by category and count rather than per record, because no title-and-abstract screening stage was logged |
| 17 | Study characteristics | Partial | Section 3, Table 1 and Figures 1 and 2 present the corpus's characteristics in aggregate, and Appendix G summarises those of the cited studies; the 1,318 included studies are not each cited and characterised in the review, and the per-study coded registry will be released with the other materials (item 27) |
| 18 | Risk of bias in studies | **Not met** | See item 11 |
| 19 | Results of individual studies | Partial | Every cited study's coded row and structured summary, keyed by identifier, will be released with the other materials (item 27); no per-study effect estimate with its precision is tabulated, because no pooling is performed |
| 20a-d | Results of syntheses | Met as SWiM | Sections 4 to 7 report each admitted claim with its supporting studies, independence status, scope and support grade; Figure C1 reports the attrition from candidate claims to admitted claims. SWiM item 8 |
| 21 | Reporting biases | **Not met** | See item 14 |
| 22 | Certainty of evidence | Partial | Table 4; see item 15 |
| 23a | Discussion: interpretation | Met | Sections 8.1, 8.2 and 8.4 |
| 23b | Discussion: limitations of the evidence | Met | Section 8.3 |
| 23c | Discussion: limitations of the review process | Met | Section 8.3; Supplementary Note S3 records the verification history including its failures. SWiM item 9 |
| 23d | Discussion: implications | Met | Sections 7, 8.2 and 8.4 |
| 24a | Registration | **Not met** | No protocol was registered |
| 24b | Protocol access | **Not met** | No protocol document was written before the work began; the companion skeleton document (`literature_review_skeleton.md` in the repository) and this Supplementary Material document the design retrospectively |
| 24c | Amendments to the protocol | **Not met** | Not applicable in the absence of a registered protocol; material scope and method changes are dated in Appendix C instead |
| 25 | Support and funding | Met | Front matter: no specific funding |
| 26 | Competing interests | Met | Front matter: none declared |
| 27 | Availability of data, code and materials | Pending | The registry, the structured summaries, all five claim ledgers, the adjudication worksheets, the figures and every generating script will be released at https://github.com/misogare/PAPERINGO-public (Section 2, data availability); none of them is publicly available at the current version |
