- **C-254** (z = 2.49): Two cohort studies of older adults with MCI disagree on antihypertensive use and progression to dementia. Among 237 Taiwanese veterans (P-1157), baseline antihypertensive exposure carried an HR of 0.59 (0.37-0.93). The difference is beyond combined uncertainty (z = 2.49, log scale). P-999 compares treated with untreated hypertensives and adjusts for statin and metformin use, while P-1157's unexposed group includes normotensive patients. P-999's own statin-user stratum (0.84, 0.80-0.88) does not differ from P-1157 beyond uncertainty (z = 1.50). These differences are candidate explanations, none shown to be sufficient.
- **C-258** (z = 2.16): Two meta-analyses of aerobic exercise against control in older adults with MCI report effects on cognition that differ beyond combined uncertainty. P-680's broader 'aerobic' class (it includes a music-accompanied trial and a dance trial), its no-exercise comparators, its composite outcome, its exclusion of amnestic trials and its heterogeneity are candidate explanations, none shown to be sufficient.
- **C-261** (z = 2.33): For predicting incident MCI in cognitively normal Chinese community adults, P-824's self-report risk score reached an AUC of 0.870 at 3 years (0.861 at 5 years) in its CHARLS development cohort, against 0.785 for P-981's model with cognitive tests on 72 converters and 849 stable participants (z = 2.3-2.6; neither paper prints an interval). The outcome label, education acting as both predictor and label determinant, the predictor sets, cohort age and event count are candidate explanations, none shown to be sufficient.
- **C-265** (z = 4.95): In cognitively unimpaired older adults, plasma p-tau217 with amyloid positivity carried an HR for incident MCI of 7.81 (95% CI 3.92-15.59) in the family-history-enriched PREVENT-AD cohort (P-1403), against an HR of 1.07 (0.73-1.56) for high vs low serum p-tau217 in the SNAC-K community sample (P-1421).
