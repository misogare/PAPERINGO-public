Four risk-of-bias appraisals were drafted on 2026-10-23 by a model reader (Claude Fable 5.1) at the author's request,
after the four studies were cited for the first time in Section 4.1.5 of the thesis (Table 4, the measures compared with
hippocampal volume on the same participants). They follow `../ROB_BRIEF.md` and the format of the accepted records in the
parent folder, and each `signalling` quotation was checked mechanically against the mapped PDF (`quote_check` on each
record: 75 of 82 quotations located; the rest are split by page breaks or carry reference numbers the extraction dropped).

The four records were held in this folder, and not counted, until the author had reviewed them. The author reviewed and
accepted them on 2026-10-23 (`review_status` and the `operator_review_2026_10_23` block on each record; the drafting-stage
status is kept under `review_status_pre_2026_10_23`). They were then moved into `../` (the counted folder), the appraisal
worksheet, the convergence sensitivity table and Figure 7 of the thesis were regenerated, and the thesis counts changed from
193 to 197 appraised of the 210 cited corpus studies (Sections 3.9 and 9, Appendix G, Table G1). This README stays as the
record of the drafting date; the folder holds no appraisal records.
