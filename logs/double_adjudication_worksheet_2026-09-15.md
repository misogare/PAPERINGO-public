# Second-reader adjudication worksheet (2026-09-15)

45 ledger records in random order: a 25% stratified sample of the records cited in literature_review.md (29 records the first reader admitted) mixed with 16 records the first reader rejected or demoted in the same ledgers. The first-reader verdicts are withheld (they are in `double_adjudication_key_2026-09-15.csv`; do not open it before scoring).

**Instructions.** For each item, read the claim and open the cited summary blocks in summaries.md (grep `^## Paper N:`), and the PDF where a block is thin. Record one verdict per item in the CSV column `second_reader_verdict`: `admit` (the claim is supported by the cited studies as stated), `rescope` (supported only with a narrower scope; say what in the note), or `reject` (not supported, or the cited studies do not show it). Then run `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8 python scripts/score_double_adjudication.py`, which reports raw agreement and Cohen's kappa (rescope counted as admit, and separately as reject) between first and second reader, and lists every disagreement. A kappa on ~44 items has a wide interval; it is a pilot, and the disagreements matter more than the coefficient.
