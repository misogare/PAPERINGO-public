# Individual re-check of the 108 en-bloc gap promotions (2026-09-22)

**Why.** The 108 gap records promoted from the candidate backlog on 2026-09-14/15 (78 of them as one block) entered the ledger with the operator's recorded caveat that their absence claims had not been individually re-checked against the current corpus. A pre-submission reviewer asked for exactly that re-check before submission.

**Method.** Twelve read-only verifier agents (9 records each) plus one conceptual-duplicate agent. Per record: (1) ANCHOR - the first claiming paper's summary block located by header and checked to support the record's clause with a verbatim, line-referenced quote; (2) CLOSURE - 3 to 5 distinctive terms grepped over the whole of summaries.md (including the post-window blocks P-1263..P-1428), up to three candidate blocks read, verdict STILL-OPEN or POSSIBLY-CLOSED with a verbatim quote; (3) FORM - corpus-relative researchable gap vs single-paper limitations list. 374 verbatim quote checks in total. The agents were advisory: no status changed, no gate verdict issued. One anchor ambiguity (P-1006's AUROC composition) was resolved by reading the source PDF. This file is the audit record; the batch reports are archived in the session scratchpad.
